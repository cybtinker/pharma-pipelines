Filled marks have happened; outlined marks are expected. The dot beside a pipeline drug is its chance of success: green likely, amber unknown, red likely failure. Bars are guided windows (a quarter or half-year). Tap a drug for details and sources.

How we rate chance of success
  • Likely Approved, filed with regulators, or a Phase 3 trial met its main goal.
  • Unknown Phase 3 or earlier trials are still running, or the data is mixed.
  • Likely failure A trial missed its main goal, the program was stopped, or the company wrote down its value.

These are our own judgments from trial results and company reports, not company guidance. Each drug's reason and sources are in its details (tap it on the timeline) and in the launches table below.
